The MSRP-ETB paper trade

Hypothesis to track: obtain an Elite Trainer Box at MSRP, pay tax, then measure what a later sale would leave after fees and shipping. MSRP availability is not assured. The historical report assumes in-store entry with no inbound shipping; it does not model stock rationing, storage, damage, or time spent sourcing.

Historical MSRP-entry returns, means over products; not cumulative account returns. Source: PriceCharting, derived statistics, accessed 2026-11-04.

The two-month loose-pack result is +203%, an unstable early result that does not persist at the longer horizons. The table includes every reported horizon. The 24-36-month gains reflect one boom (2025-26). No ETB-only sample counts, confidence intervals, or adjusted p-values are supplied by this report. Its total universe contains 67 ETBs, but not all have each holding horizon.
