Methods and limits

Sources and coverage

TCGplayer prices via tcgcsv (accessed 2026-11-04): our weekly panel runs 2024-02-08 through 2026-10-29, with separate product and printing identifiers. MTG, Pokémon and Riftbound have different coverage; Riftbound has a shorter history. Market, low, mid, high and Direct Low are price fields, not executed orders in our account. We do not treat market prices or low listings as a promise that a sale will fill.

PriceCharting (accessed 2026-11-04): monthly sold-price history for modern English Pokémon sealed. Our importer uses its sealed-price series. We publish our own aggregates with source credit and a link, not product-level monthly prices or raw downloads. Rights for a paid product remain UNVERIFIED; see PriceCharting’s terms (accessed 2026-11-04). This is not a claim of permission to resell its data.

The sealed report covers 164 standard-MSRP products across 30 sets: 67 ETBs, 50 packs, 27 booster bundles, and 20 booster boxes. Each horizon uses the products with available outcomes, so denominators change. We did not recompute these analyses for this site; figures are copied from Honest Holo’s reports reviewed 2026-11-04.

Cost model

These are backtest assumptions, not a current fee quote or a tax calculation for every buyer. The model uses 6% purchase tax. Let sale price be P:

commission     = min(rate * P, $75)
processing     = 2.5% * (P * 1.06) + $0.30
seller net     = P - commission - processing - outbound shipping
market cost    = entry price * 1.06 + inbound shipping
MSRP cost      = MSRP * 1.06                  (assumed in-store purchase)
net return     = seller net / entry cost - 1

For the weekly card backtest, entry price = max(market, low listing). Commission is 10.25% before 2026-02-10 and 10.75% afterward. The sealed MSRP analysis instead uses a fixed 10.75% rate for every exit, including historical exits. Both use the $75 cap and processing formula above.

Shipping is assumed per item, on both entry and exit unless MSRP entry:

Product Price threshold Modeled shipping
Single Below $50 $1
Single $50 or more $5
Sealed Below $150 $8
Sealed $150 or more $20

No allowance for storage, insurance, returns, damage, sourcing time, or inability to sell. These omissions can make results too optimistic.

Sealed MSRP study

Source of record: tcg-backtest/reports/pokemon_msrp.md and src/msrp.py. Entry assumes MSRP plus tax at release, with no inbound shipping. Market entry uses month-one market price plus tax and shipping. Exit is measured at release plus 2, 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, not entry month plus the holding horizon. Thus a “12-month” market-entry result exits twelve months after release.

The inherited price lookup can use the last available price in the preceding two months for an exit; the month-one entry lookup looks back one month. A missing exit is excluded, so these are available-outcome aggregates, not a complete portfolio. Product release timing uses known set release months where available and earliest observed sealed-sale month otherwise.

The MSRP reference assumptions are $4.49 per pack, $26.94 per bundle, $49.99 per standard ETB, $59.99 per Pokémon Center ETB, and $143.64 per booster box. These are the report’s reference assumptions, UNVERIFIED as per-product historical MSRPs. The box sensitivity uses $161.64. Products with special MSRPs are excluded. Availability at MSRP is not modeled, so this is an optimistic entry scenario.

Overall net returns first average products within each set, then average sets. The 95% intervals resample sets 5,000 times; the five holding-horizon p-values receive Holm correction. The product-type table instead averages products. It has no subgroup uncertainty estimates. Do not attach the overall CI to ETBs. These retrospective descriptive cohorts are not a forward-deployment test.

The 24-36-month gains include one boom (2025-26). Different ages also exit in different calendar markets. Set age and calendar regime are not cleanly separated.

What failed

The model/rule study uses forward-chaining training, never random train/test splits. Features read only data available at the decision date; training labels must have matured. Models refit every eight weeks, and calibration uses matured out-of-sample predictions. The holdout starts 2026-03-05.

The primary rule selects up to 25 items with calibrated profit probability at least 0.5 and holds cash if nothing qualifies. Primary scoring assigns −100% to a pick with no future price in the horizon. A fallback price mark is not proof of sale. Each game is tested at four and thirteen weeks.

The tested claim was positive mean weekly net return. Inference uses time-block bootstrap and Newey-West uncertainty, reporting the larger p-value, then Holm across the six game-by-horizon tests. None passed at 5%. Source: tcg-backtest/reports/summary.md. That fails our requirement for claiming a profitable buy rule. It does not prove every possible strategy loses.

High rank correlation mostly reflected lagging market prices catching up with listings. It did not establish a tradeable edge. Selected-pick calibration was poor despite favorable aggregate classification metrics. We publish neither the models as buy signals nor the ETB subgroup mean as a forecast.

Evidence needed

A dated, pre-registered forward paper portfolio would need positive net returns with uncertainty that excludes zero, after costs, missing outcomes, and a fixed multiple-testing policy. We would compare strategies using paired differences, not differences between independently eyeballed intervals. An interval spanning zero remains inconclusive. No such forward result exists here yet.

Sell Check demand protocol

The frozen R8/DECISIONS design tests signup probability 1% versus 3%, capped at 600 unique waitlist visitors. Build boundary: s >= 2.48 + 0.0182*n; stop boundary: s <= -1.39 + 0.0182*n, where s is signups and n is visitors. Counts are evaluated sequentially, not by repeatedly running fixed-sample p-tests. At the cap without a boundary crossing, report inconclusive.

A demand signal also needs signups from at least three distinct src tags. Fewer than 100 visitors by week six means no distribution, not no demand. Changing copy resets the cohort. The later paid validation requires at least 50 signups and 10 card-authorized pre-orders within two weeks, after the data-license review clears, including written permission from TCGplayer and PriceCharting and a lawyer’s review. The waitlist is a funnel meter, not authorization to build or charge for a product. No money is collected on this site. B5 owns analysis and counting; the local beacon is unconfigured, so this build reports no visitor denominator.
