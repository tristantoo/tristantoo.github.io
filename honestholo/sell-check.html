Sell Check

A proposed Pokémon sealed tool for estimating proceeds after selling costs.

This doesn’t exist yet. We’re checking whether enough people want it before we build it.

What it would do

Pokémon sealed first:

  • For every sealed product in print: MSRP, today’s TCGplayer market price, and what you’d actually net after TCGplayer fees and shipping if you sold today.
  • An alert when something you own crosses a net price you set.
  • What past sets did in the months after release, with how many sets that’s based on. History, not a forecast.

What it won’t do

  • Tell you what to buy. Our buy-signal models failed out-of-sample testing, so we don’t sell them.
  • Promise profit. In our data, sealed Pokémon bought at market price in a set’s first month was down 33-47% on average at six to twelve months after release, net of selling costs. Those figures average sets, not dollars invested.

Hypothetical paper trade, no money invested. Historical results use fixed entry/exit rules and include modeled tax, fees and shipping (rules and costs). MSRP entry is assumed and often isn’t achievable. No forward positions are registered; forward rules and the valuation schedule must be fixed before the first position.

Credit: Honest Holo aggregate analysis of PriceCharting sold-price history (accessed 2026-11-04), from pokemon_msrp.md. See the losses and methods.
