Key findings

−47%

Mean net return at six months after release for standard Pokémon sealed bought at month-one market prices, averaged across sets.

Source: PriceCharting, pokemon_msrp.md, reviewed 2026-11-04.

−33%

The same month-one market entry lost a third on average at twelve months after release; entry price and selling costs matter.

Source: PriceCharting, pokemon_msrp.md, reviewed 2026-11-04.

+117%

Historical 24-month ETB mean net return with hypothetical MSRP entry, averaged over products, reflects one boom (2025-26), not a forecast.

Source: PriceCharting, pokemon_msrp.md, reviewed 2026-11-04; no subgroup CI or significance test reported.

−54%

Loose packs bought at hypothetical MSRP lost more than half at six months after release, a mean net return over products.

Source: PriceCharting, pokemon_msrp.md, reviewed 2026-11-04; all horizons.

−66.5%

MTG singles’ median set price change at 104 weeks was negative versus week two, before costs, across 15 sets.

Source: TCGplayer via tcgcsv, mtg_lifecycle.md, reviewed 2026-11-05.

+273%

Pokémon sealed’s median price change at 48 months versus month two, before costs, covers just five sets and one boom (2025-26).

Source: PriceCharting, pokemon_sealed_long.md, reviewed 2026-11-05; changing cohorts.

Hypothetical paper trade, no money invested. Historical results use fixed entry/exit rules and include modeled tax, fees and shipping (rules and costs). MSRP entry is assumed and often isn’t achievable. No forward positions are registered; forward rules and the valuation schedule must be fixed before the first position.

Sell Check remains a proposal with a waitlist placeholder. Data-license review is open: no card images, official game logos or paid data sales. Read the Sell Check proposal.

How we test

Honest Holo is Tristan Kennedy’s trading-card price research for Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and Riftbound: weekly movers, lifecycle summaries and a paper-trade scoreboard. No primary test passed: the buy-rule tests did not establish positive net returns after Holm correction across six game-by-horizon tests. Model rankings did not establish a tradeable edge. No buy signals; these are historical analyses, not today’s executable prices. Read the test protocol and limitations.
